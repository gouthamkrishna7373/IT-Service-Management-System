How to Fix Common IT Problems

What should I do if my computer is slow?

Close unused applications, restart the computer, and check available storage.

What should I do if the internet is not working?

Check your Wi-Fi connection, restart your router if appropriate, and contact IT support.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use your organization's approved password-reset process or contact IT support.
